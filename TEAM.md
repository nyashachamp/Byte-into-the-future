# 🐝 Byte into the Future!

**Building intelligent, responsible technology for the communities of tomorrow.**

Welcome to the team behind **Civic Shield**, an AI-assisted public-service risk assessment and decision-support project developed through the **IBM SkillsBuild AI Experiential Learning Lab**.

## Our mission

We explore how **artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, cloud computing, and data analytics** can work together to solve meaningful problems. Our goal is to build practical, accountable tools that help people understand risks, make informed decisions, and improve essential services.

We believe innovation should be **human-centered, evidence-driven, secure by design, and accessible**.

## Our first project: Civic Shield

Civic Shield explores how specialized AI agents can support public-sector cybersecurity workflows: investigating synthetic security findings, correlating technical risks with public-service dependencies, prioritizing findings, and producing evidence-based recommendations and human handoffs.

The team worked with **Python, IBM watsonx Orchestrate, IBM Bob, a Streamlit dashboard, synthetic test cases, and GitHub** throughout prototyping and testing. The demonstration is a controlled prototype—not a claim of deployment in a live government environment.

## What we bring together

- **Cybersecurity and risk assessment** — identifying threats, assessing controls, and keeping human oversight in the loop
- **AI engineering and orchestration** — exploring specialized agents, tool workflows, and decision support
- **Data analysis and visualization** — turning risk evidence into understandable dashboards and reports
- **Cloud and application development** — building, integrating, and testing practical software
- **Research, documentation, and collaboration** — making our work transparent and reproducible

## Our values

**Curiosity.** We investigate how technology works, and why.

**Responsibility.** Security, privacy, accountability, and human judgment matter.

**Collaboration.** We combine complementary perspectives and learn together.

**Verification.** We distinguish an idea, a simulation, and a tested result.

**Public impact.** Technology should serve real people and real needs.
